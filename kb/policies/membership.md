## Plans and pricing

Larkspur Plus costs $49 per year or $5.99 per month. New members can start with a 30-day free trial, available once per customer. The membership renews automatically at the end of each billing period until it is cancelled.

## Shipping and returns benefits

Members get free 2-day shipping on every order with no minimum, and free return labels on every return. Members also get a longer return window: 60 days for most items instead of 30, and 30 days for electronics instead of 15. Advance exchanges, where the replacement ships before the original comes back, are available only to members.

## Warranty and rewards benefits

Members get one extra year of warranty coverage on electronics bought while the membership is active. Members also earn 5% back on every purchase as Larkspur Rewards credit. Rewards credit expires 12 months after it is earned, cannot be used to buy gift cards, and has no cash value.

## Early access

Members get access to seasonal sales 24 hours before everyone else, plus member-only deals posted on the first Monday of each month.

## Household sharing

A member can share shipping and returns benefits with one other person who lives at the same address. Rewards credit and the extended electronics warranty stay with the account holder and are not shared.

## Cancelling a membership

You can cancel at any time from Account > Membership. An annual membership cancelled within its first 30 days is refunded in full if no member benefits were used. After 30 days, annual memberships are not refunded and benefits continue until the end of the paid year. Monthly memberships are never refunded; benefits continue until the end of the current month. Cancelling during the free trial costs nothing.
