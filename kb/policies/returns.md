## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date shown in your order history. The window is counted in calendar days, not business days, and it closes at 11:59 pm Eastern Time on the last day. Larkspur Plus members get a 60-day window on the same items. If an order arrived in several packages, each package has its own window that starts on the day that package was delivered.

## Electronics

Headphones, keyboards, monitors, speakers, smart-home devices and other electronics have a shorter window: 15 days from delivery (30 days for Larkspur Plus members). They must come back with every accessory, cable and manual that was in the box. An opened electronic item that is not defective is charged a 15% restocking fee, deducted from the refund. Unopened items in their factory seal, and items that turn out to be defective, are never charged the fee.

## Items that cannot be returned

Some items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged: anything marked "Final Sale" on the product page (usually clearance), gift cards and e-gift cards, personalized or engraved items, perishable goods such as coffee beans and snacks, and opened personal-care products. Final-sale items are still covered by the warranty if they are defective.

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Shoes must be tried on indoors only; soles that show outdoor wear are not accepted. Items that arrive used, damaged by the customer or missing parts may receive only a partial refund, or may be sent back to the customer at their expense.

## How to start a return

Start every return in the Returns Portal under Orders > Return an item. You will receive a return authorization (RMA) number and instructions. The RMA number is valid for 14 days, and the package must be dropped off with the carrier within those 14 days. Packages sent without an RMA number are refused at the warehouse and returned to the sender.

## Return shipping costs

If the item is defective, damaged on arrival or not what you ordered, we email a free prepaid return label. For all other returns, a prepaid label costs $6.95 and the fee is deducted from your refund; you may also ship the item back yourself with any tracked service. Larkspur Plus members get free return labels on every return.

## Holiday returns

Items purchased between November 15 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year. This extended window does not apply to electronics, which keep their normal 15-day window (30 days for Larkspur Plus members), or to final-sale items.
