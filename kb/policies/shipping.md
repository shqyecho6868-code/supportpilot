## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, or on weekends and US holidays, ship the next business day. Large furniture items ship separately from the rest of the order and may take 2 extra business days to leave the warehouse.

## Shipping options and costs

- Standard shipping: 5 to 7 business days, $5.99, free on orders of $50 or more.
- Expedited shipping: 2 to 3 business days, $14.99.
- Overnight shipping: next business day, $29.99. Overnight orders must be placed by 12 pm (noon) Eastern Time and cannot be delivered to PO boxes.

Larkspur Plus members get free 2-day shipping on every order with no minimum.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Puerto Rico and APO/FPO addresses. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico use standard shipping only, take 7 to 12 business days, and are not eligible for overnight delivery. Outside the US we ship only to Canada and the United Kingdom: international delivery takes 10 to 20 business days and any customs duties or import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the order ships and appears in your order history. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. Orders that ship in several boxes get one tracking number per box.

## Changing a shipping address

The shipping address can be changed while the order is still pending, that is, before it ships. Once an order has shipped, we cannot change the address; you can ask the carrier to redirect the package, but carrier redirects are not guaranteed and may cost extra.

## Lost packages

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, or the package does not arrive within 30 days of the expected delivery date, contact support. We will open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping charges. Claims must be reported within 30 days of the expected delivery date.

## Damaged deliveries

If a package arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery and include photos of the box and the item. We will send a replacement at no cost or refund the item, including the shipping charge. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed, because the carrier may ask to inspect it.
