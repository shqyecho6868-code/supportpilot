## Exchange window

You can exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product within 45 days of delivery. This is longer than the 30-day return window, so an item that can no longer be returned may still be exchanged. Exchanges for a different product are not possible; return the item and place a new order instead.

## What can be exchanged

The item must be unused, with tags attached and in its original packaging. Final-sale items, gift cards and personalized items cannot be exchanged. Electronics can only be exchanged for the same model, and only because of a defect reported within 15 days of delivery; after that, defects are handled under the warranty.

## How exchanges work

Start an exchange in the Returns Portal and choose "Exchange" instead of "Refund". We email a free prepaid label, and the replacement ships as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier. Shipping is free both ways on the first exchange of an item.

## Limits

Each item can be exchanged once. If the replacement also needs to change, it must be returned for a refund under the Returns Policy and a new order placed. Return shipping for that second change follows the normal return label rules.

## Price differences

If the replacement costs more than the original item, you pay the difference before the replacement ships. If it costs less, the difference is refunded to your original payment method on the normal refund timeline.

## Out-of-stock replacements

If the size or color you want is out of stock, we offer store credit for the full item price or a refund to the original payment method. We do not hold exchanges open waiting for a restock.

## Advance exchanges

Larkspur Plus members can request an advance exchange: we ship the replacement first, before the original comes back. We place a temporary hold for the item's value on your card. If the original item is not received within 21 days, the hold is charged.
