## Limited warranty

Every product sold by Larkspur Supply is covered by a limited warranty against manufacturing defects for 1 year from the delivery date, unless a longer period is listed below. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. It applies to the original purchaser and is not transferable.

## Furniture coverage

Office chairs, desks and other furniture have longer coverage: 5 years on the frame, 2 years on mechanisms such as gas lifts, tilt controls and casters, and 1 year on upholstery, foam and fabric. The longer coverage applies only to furniture.

## Electronics coverage

Electronics are covered for 1 year from delivery. Larkspur Plus members get an extra year of coverage on electronics, for 2 years in total, as long as their membership was active on the purchase date. Batteries are covered for 6 months, or until they hold less than 70% of their original capacity, whichever comes first.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover accidental damage such as drops and cracked screens, liquid or water damage, normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage that does not affect function, damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications, or products bought from third-party sellers. Accidental damage can be covered by a CareGuard protection plan.

## Making a warranty claim

Submit a claim from your order history with proof of purchase, a description of the problem, and photos or a short video. Include the serial number for electronics. If we approve the claim, we email a free shipping label. We repair, replace or refund the product at our discretion, normally within 10 business days of receiving it.

## Replacement coverage

A product that is repaired or replaced under warranty is covered for the rest of the original warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer. Refunds for warranty claims are issued to the original payment method.

## CareGuard protection plans

CareGuard is an optional paid plan that adds accidental-damage protection, including drops, spills and cracked screens, for 2 or 3 years from purchase. It must be added within 30 days of buying the product. A CareGuard plan can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund; after that, cancellations receive a prorated refund.
