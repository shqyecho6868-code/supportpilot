## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Larkspur gift cards and Klarna. We do not accept personal checks, money orders, cash on delivery, cryptocurrency or wire transfers. All prices are in US dollars; banks may charge their own foreign transaction fees for cards issued outside the US.

## Klarna pay-in-4

Klarna splits a purchase into 4 interest-free payments made every two weeks. It is available on orders between $35 and $1,000 for customers in the US. Klarna runs its own approval check at checkout, and late fees are set by Klarna, not by Larkspur Supply.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in several packages, each package is charged separately when it ships. A cancelled order's authorization hold is released by your bank, usually within 3 to 7 business days.

## Gift cards

Larkspur gift cards never expire and have no fees. You can use up to 5 gift cards on one order and pay any remaining balance with another payment method. Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards, cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by law, and lost or stolen gift cards can only be replaced with the original purchase receipt.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails when the order ships, for example because the card has expired, we email you and hold the order for 48 hours. If the payment method is not updated within 48 hours, the order is cancelled automatically.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated from the shipping address, not the billing address, and is shown at checkout before you pay. Tax-exempt organizations can apply for an exemption certificate through support before ordering; exemptions cannot be applied to orders that have already shipped.

## Payment security

Card numbers are encrypted and handled by our payment processor; Larkspur Supply never stores full card numbers. Our support team will never ask for your full card number, security code or password in chat, email or by phone.
