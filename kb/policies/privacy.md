## Information we collect

We collect the information you give us, such as your name, email, shipping addresses and order history, and information collected automatically when you use the site, such as device type, pages viewed and approximate location. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

## How long we keep data

Order and payment records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and accounting rules. Support chat transcripts are kept for 90 days and then deleted. Marketing preferences are kept for as long as your account is open. Full card numbers are never stored; our payment processor keeps a token instead.

## Accessing and exporting your data

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you from Account > Privacy or by emailing privacy@larkspur.example. We verify your identity first and deliver the export within 30 days of the verified request.

## Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data. Deletion is completed within 45 days of the verified request. Some records cannot be deleted before their retention period ends because the law requires us to keep them, such as order and payment records; these are kept but no longer used for marketing.

## Marketing emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link at the bottom of any message or from Account > Notifications. Opt-outs are processed within 10 business days. Order confirmations, shipping updates and other service emails are still sent after you unsubscribe.

## Cookies

The site uses essential cookies that are needed for checkout, analytics cookies that help us improve the site, and advertising cookies. You can turn analytics and advertising cookies off at any time under Cookie Preferences in the site footer. Essential cookies cannot be turned off.

## Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we delete it.
