## When a refund is issued

Once a return reaches our warehouse it is inspected within 2 business days. If it passes inspection, the refund is issued within 3 business days and you receive a confirmation email. The refund date depends on inspection, not on when you shipped the package, so tracked shipping helps you follow its progress.

## Refund timing by payment method

After we issue a refund, how quickly you see it depends on how you paid:

- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 10 business days for your bank to post it.
- PayPal: 3 to 5 business days.
- Apple Pay and Google Pay: same as the underlying card, 5 to 10 business days.
- Klarna: Klarna adjusts your payment plan within up to 14 days.
- Store credit: available instantly.

## Where refunds go

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund to a different card, a bank account or another person. If the original card has been closed or replaced, the bank normally forwards the money to the new card; if it is returned to us, we issue store credit instead. Purchases paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit.

## Shipping charges

Original shipping charges are not refundable, except when the return is our fault: a defective item, an item damaged in transit, or a wrong item sent. The $6.95 return label fee, when it applies, is deducted from the refund total.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund may be given when an item comes back used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts, accessories or original packaging. The deduction depends on the item's condition and can be up to 50% of the item price. The 15% restocking fee on opened, non-defective electronics is separate from these deductions.

## Price adjustments

If the price of an item drops within 14 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference once per order. Price adjustments are not given for flash sales, clearance items, or competitors' prices. The difference is refunded to the original payment method on the same timeline as any other refund.

## Cancelled orders

An order that is cancelled before it ships is refunded in full. Because cards are only authorized when you order and charged when the order ships, cancelling a pending order releases the authorization hold, which usually disappears from your statement within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.
